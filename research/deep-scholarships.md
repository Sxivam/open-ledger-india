# Finance funding verification — 2 November 2026

The companion JSON has 11 records: five open, one rolling, one upcoming and four watchlist resources. All study/research locations are India; international exam providers are included only as qualifications accessible from India. No overseas relocation award is included. Every record has its own eligibility and funding caveat.

## Verification and status decisions

- CFA Access is upcoming on this audit date. Do not count it as open before its published opening date. The current [Access page](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/scholarships/access) is the primary timing source; [rules](https://www.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/programs/cfa-program/access-scholarship-rules.pdf) explain registration restrictions.
- The [Student portal](https://cfai.smapply.io/prog/cfa_program_student_scholarship/) and [Professor portal](https://cfai.smapply.io/prog/cfa_program_professor_scholarship/) explicitly identify the 2026–27 cycle. Preserve their EDT deadlines through `deadlineAt`; do not silently interpret those clocks as IST. [Student information](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/scholarships/student) and [Professor information](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/scholarships/professor) provide additional conditions. The professor information page conflicts with its live portal about the closing date; the record discloses this and recommends applying earlier.
- [ICAI's current FAQ](https://icai-call-sahayata.icai.org/assets/pdf/Scholarship-FAQ.pdf) was downloaded and the first-page award table visually inspected. It is updated May 2026. Do not substitute the older January 2025 FAQ and its superseded amounts/income ceiling.
- Kerala has a dated 2026–27 window in its [official scheme table](https://www.mwdscholarship.kerala.gov.in/admin/viewguidelines); the [government education portal](https://careerprayanam.education.kerala.gov.in/home/Scholarships) corroborates funding and income terms. Its linked [English guidelines](https://mwdscholarship.kerala.gov.in/storage/guidelines/DZpEyCZyUqsYQL4GVwrIl7r9j3POQh8Ronmb9Lpw.pdf) still bear a prior-year title. The card does not pretend this conflict is resolved.
- BITS' [departmental news item](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/news/call-for-ph-d-applications-january-2027/) links an [official poster](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/phd-bits-appl-2027.jpg), visually inspected. Funding and general entry requirements were cross-checked against the current [central admissions page](https://admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in/PhD/phd.html). Department evergreen pages show older fellowship figures, so those were not used.
- ICSSR's [current second-call announcement](https://icssr.org/announcements/2nd-call-applications-icssrs-senior-fellowship-2026) supersedes an older search snippet with different dates. The [scheme page](https://www.icssr.org/senior-fellowship) supplies award and eligibility rules. This is a senior-researcher social-science route, relevant to appropriate public-finance proposals; it is not a finance-only graduate fellowship.
- ICSI is a watchlist entry: the [student-services directory](https://www.icsi.edu/media/webmodules/Student_Services_links.pdf) identifies the scheme, but [SEFT rules](https://www.icsi.edu/media/webmodules/student/SEFTGuidelines202201072024.pdf), [March 2026 student journal](https://www.icsi.edu/media/webmodules/DAR/SCS_e_Journal_Mar_2026.pdf) and application links were inconsistently retrievable, with final rechecks returning 404. Current form acceptance was not verified. No open badge is justified.
- ICMAI's [financial-aid directory](https://icmai.in/ClntStudents/Student_FinancialAids_Scholarships) remains useful, but linked PDFs returned access errors and a current application cycle was not established. GARP's [FAQ](https://www.garp.org/frm/frequently-asked-questions) is a nomination route, not a public scholarship call; its [partner list](https://www.garp.org/about/outreach/academic-partners) must be checked at programme level.
- NISM's [PGDM page](https://www.nism.ac.in/revamp-nism/long-term-programs/post-graduate-diploma-in-management-securities-markets-pgdm-sm/) has a past admission deadline. Retained only as a next-intake watch; a generic application CTA is not evidence of a new open cohort.

## Scope and checks

The records use 11 primary `sourceUrl` pages across 10 hosts, with the supporting official pages linked above. JSON syntax, record IDs, required fields and India-only geography were validated. This is a vetted set, not a claim that every possible institutional scholarship has been discovered. Closed RBI faculty and past CFA cycles are excluded from live results. Generic school scholarships sponsored by banks were not padded into a finance-career catalogue.
