## Navigation and validation

“New this refresh” filters by the original ledger-addition date and works with
search, eligibility, opportunity type, shortlist and shared URLs. Rechecking an
older record never makes it new. The recently-added sort uses known publication
dates to order additions within the same refresh. The interface explains that
addition date and publication date differ.

Validation covers all previous IDs exactly once, required source/evidence and
eligibility fields, India scope, unique IDs and new-source URLs, expired dates,
removal application, preserved discovery dates, and new-filter results. The
historical date/time-zone, funding and watchlist tests remain in place.

The scope includes India jobs and in-person programmes, plus existing globally
accessible funding/learning for people in India. This is a broad researched
snapshot, not a claim to include every finance vacancy. No applications,
recruiter messages or candidate information were submitted.
